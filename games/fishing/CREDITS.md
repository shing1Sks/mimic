# Tidecast credits

No third-party assets. Everything you see and hear is made in code:

- Visuals: drawn at runtime with three.js r149 (MIT, `games/lib/three.min.js`): the sky, sea shader,
  dock, anglers, rods, fish models and their canvas-painted skins.
- Sound and music: synthesised at runtime with WebAudio (`audio.js`): the sea, gulls, the slow
  pentatonic guitar-and-pad bed, reel clicks, drag, splashes, the foghorn.
- Shared Mimic libraries from `games/lib/` (mimic.js, mimic-link.js, recorder.js, wandcursor.js).
