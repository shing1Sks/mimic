HOW TO PLAY

BOWLING

  • Point to aimThe ring on the pitch is your spot
  • Hold, run in, whip the arm overLet go as it comes over: early is fuller, late shorter
  • Faster arm = paceThe speed gun tells you
  • Twist the wrist = spinClockwise turns right, anticlockwise left
  • Wrist turned = swingFrom your usual grip: turned right swings right

BATTING

  • Phone = bat handleTop of the phone is the blade
  • Swing as it arrivesFastest at the ball = the middle
  • Your swing aimsIts path is where the ball goes
  • Straight bat or cross batDrive the full ones, pull the short ones
  • Keep still to leaveGentle push = defend
