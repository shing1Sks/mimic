How to fish

  1. CastHold A, flick the rod over your shoulder, let go of A as it comes forward.
  2. FeelLight ticks are nibbles. Wait. A heavy thump is the strike.
  3. StrikeJerk the rod up, hard, right on the thump.
  4. FightRod up and leaned against the run. Never reel while it runs.
  5. ReelCircle the rod tip like a crank, or hold A.
