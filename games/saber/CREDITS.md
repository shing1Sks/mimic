# Cadence Cut credits

## Music (assets/music)

All tracks downloaded from OpenGameArt.org (no login), re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis (q3) with ffmpeg.
Not modified otherwise. Beat grids and charts are generated from them by `tools/analyze.py`.

"Lantern Drift", "Copper Tide" and "Ion Runner" are original procedural songs generated in `audio.js`.

## Everything else

Sound effects are synthesised at runtime (WebAudio). Visuals are drawn in code with three.js r149
(MIT, `games/lib/three.min.js`). No other third-party assets.
